Since the 1910s

A Marton workshop with over 110 years behind it

Careys Joinery started making joinery in Marton more than a century ago. The machinery has changed, the standards have not. Today the workshop pairs skilled tradespeople with current CNC machinery, so a hand-finished job still arrives on time and on budget.

We are 100% Kiwi owned and use New Zealand-made materials from New Zealand suppliers wherever possible.
